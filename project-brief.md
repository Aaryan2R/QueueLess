# QueueLess – Project Brief

## Problem
Small businesses commonly manage customer waiting lines manually. Physical queues create crowding, uncertainty, unnecessary waiting, and additional work for staff. Informal token systems also make it difficult for owners to measure queue performance.

## Proposed Solution
QueueLess is a web-based platform where a business can create its own branded virtual queue. The business receives a QR code linked to its queue page. Customers scan the QR code, join the queue, receive a token, and monitor their position online. Staff manage the queue from a business dashboard.

## Target Businesses
- Cafés and restaurants
- Salons and beauty services
- Clinics and small healthcare service desks
- Repair/service counters
- College canteens
- Local service centers
- Other appointment-or-queue based small businesses

## Main Differentiator
QueueLess is not a single queue used by one business. It is a reusable platform where each business can create its own online queue space and customize its name, logo, theme, queue rules, and customer-facing message.

## Project Goal
Demonstrate a practical multi-role web system that combines authentication, database operations, QR codes, real-time state changes, queue logic, validation, and analytics in one coherent workflow.

## Non-Goals for MVP
- Native Android/iOS application.
- Complex payment processing.
- AI-based prediction as a mandatory component.
- Full enterprise scheduling/appointment management.
- Medical-record management for clinics.
