# QueueLess – Business Rules

## Queue Rules
1. Only an active queue accepts new customers.
2. A paused or closed queue must reject new joins with a clear message.
3. Queue capacity cannot be exceeded.
4. Token numbers are generated by the server.
5. Customers cannot choose their own token number.
6. The next customer is determined by server-side queue order.
7. Cancelled and no-show tickets do not count as waiting customers ahead.
8. Served tickets cannot return to WAITING.
9. A ticket cannot be served more than once.
10. Only authorized staff/owners can change operational ticket status.

## Status Transition Rules
```text
WAITING → CALLED
WAITING → CANCELLED
CALLED → READY
CALLED → SERVED
CALLED → NO_SHOW
READY → SERVED
READY → NO_SHOW
```

Invalid examples:
- SERVED → WAITING
- CANCELLED → CALLED
- NO_SHOW → SERVED

## Customer Rules
- Customer must provide all required fields configured by the business.
- Phone number is optional unless the business enables it as required.
- Customer can leave only while their ticket is in an eligible waiting state.

## Business Rules
- Only the business owner can change owner-level settings.
- Staff can only act on queues belonging to businesses to which they are assigned.
- Business slug must be unique.
- A business cannot publish a queue without required configuration.

## Estimate Rule
For MVP:
`estimated_wait = customers_ahead × average_service_minutes`

The estimate is informational and should be labeled as an estimate, not a guarantee.

## Data Rules
- Never store plain-text passwords.
- Do not expose customer phone numbers on public queue lists.
- Audit important status changes.
