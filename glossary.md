# QueueLess – Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Business | A customer-facing organization using QueueLess |
| Queue | A waiting line managed by a business |
| Ticket | A customer's entry in a queue |
| Token | Unique identifier displayed to a customer |
| Public Queue Page | Customer-facing page opened by QR/link |
| Owner | User who controls a business |
| Staff | Authorized employee who operates the queue |
| Wait Time | Estimated time before service |
| No-show | Customer who was called but did not appear within the allowed time |
| Multi-tenant | One platform serving multiple independent businesses |
| Queue Status | ACTIVE, PAUSED, or CLOSED |
| Ticket Status | WAITING, CALLED, READY, SERVED, NO_SHOW, or CANCELLED |
