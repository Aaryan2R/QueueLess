# QueueLess – Final Demo Script

## Demo Setup
Use one business such as a café and keep two browser windows ready:
- Window A: customer
- Window B: owner/staff

## Part 1 – Owner Setup
1. Login as business owner.
2. Show business name/logo/theme.
3. Create/select a queue named `Dine-In Queue`.
4. Show queue settings.
5. Generate/display the QR code.

## Part 2 – Customer Join
1. Open the public queue URL or scan the QR code.
2. Show the branded customer page.
3. Enter name and party size.
4. Join the queue.
5. Show generated ticket, position and estimated wait.

## Part 3 – Staff Operation
1. Return to staff dashboard.
2. Show the new customer appearing in the live queue.
3. Click `Call Next`.
4. Show the customer page changing status in real time.
5. Mark customer `Ready`/`Served`.
6. Show position/count updating.

## Part 4 – Business Controls
1. Pause the queue.
2. Open the public page and show that new customers cannot join.
3. Resume the queue.

## Part 5 – Analytics
Show:
- Customers served
- Average wait
- No-show/cancellation count
- Queue volume/history

## Final Explanation
State that the same platform can be reused by restaurants, cafés, salons, clinics and other service businesses by creating a separate branded queue space and QR code.
