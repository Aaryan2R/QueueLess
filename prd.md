# QueueLess – Product Requirements Document

## Product Name
QueueLess

## One-Line Description
A customizable web-based virtual queue platform that lets small businesses create digital queues customers can join instantly through QR codes without installing an app.

## Users
- Business owners
- Business staff
- Customers
- Platform administrators

## Product Promise
For customers: less physical waiting and better visibility.
For businesses: simpler queue operations and useful queue data.

## Primary User Journey
`Business signup → Business setup → Queue configuration → QR generation → Customer scans → Customer joins → Ticket issued → Staff operates queue → Customer receives live updates → Ticket served → Metrics recorded`

## MVP Features
- Owner authentication
- Business setup and branding
- Queue setup
- QR generation
- Customer join flow
- Ticket generation
- Position/wait estimate
- Staff live queue dashboard
- Ticket status management
- Queue pause/resume
- Queue history
- Basic analytics

## Product Constraints
- Browser based.
- No native mobile app required.
- Customer onboarding should be minimal.
- Backend is the source of truth for queue state.
- Sensitive customer information should not be public.

## Future Features
- Notification integrations
- Multiple branches
- Advanced forecasting
- Subscription plans
- Customer feedback
- PWA/offline enhancements
