# QueueLess – Roadmap

## Version 0.1 – Prototype
- Static business page.
- Basic queue form.
- Basic token display.

## Version 0.2 – Functional MVP
- Authentication.
- Business creation.
- Queue CRUD.
- QR generation.
- Customer join/leave.
- Staff queue controls.

- Wait estimates.
- Status history.
- Basic analytics.

## Version 0.4 – Polished Semester Demo
- Business customization.
- Responsive UI.
- Security hardening.
- Test suite.
- Deployment.

## Future Product Version
- Multiple branches.
- Notifications.
- Advanced wait prediction.
- Subscription billing.
- PWA/offline support.
- Deeper analytics.
